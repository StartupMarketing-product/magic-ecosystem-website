In development

The mortgage comes together while the buyer picks the apartment.

Buyers don't leave to shop for a bank on their own. Magic prepares the file, a licensed participant takes it to several banks, and the sales team sees the status for every buyer.

The system collects income, equity, apartment price and documents once. The buyer and the sales team see up front what amount the banks are likely to consider. The credit decision is made by the bank. At three, five and seven years, Magic reminds the buyer that terms can be revisited.

Developer / Buyer

Developer

Deals that spend less time waiting on the bank, and more buyers who make it to signing.

Buyer

An estimated amount and payment up front, with terms from several banks side by side.

Boundaries

Magic doesn't issue loans or accept funds.

The credit decision and the money stay with the licensed party, and settlement runs through the buyer's bank.
