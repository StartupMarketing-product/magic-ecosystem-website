How the work runs

  1. 1Process and spend review
  2. 2First services selected
  3. 3Launch
  4. 4Measured on your numbers
  5. 5Expansion

The setup is built around your company. What delivers results fastest goes live first, and the rest is added based on what the measurements show. At this step there's no need to change suppliers or buy anything through Magic.

Control

  • An agent acts only within its role and permissions.
  • Every action is logged with its reason, and an action inside Magic can be reversed.
  • Company data stays inside your environment, and a ban on training models on it is set in the contract.
