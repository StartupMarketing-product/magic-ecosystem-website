Computer vision compares the site with the building model and the schedule. Magic turns each deviation into an order, a delivery, a budget line and a choice between two amounts.

Three roles

Data provider

Capture, recognition, percent complete, delay forecast.

Magic

Need, product, price, purchasing, logistics, financing, budget.

Project lead

Makes the decision.

Magic is designed to connect to the construction monitoring system a developer already uses, or to its in-house system. The developer grants data access under its contract with that system's provider.
