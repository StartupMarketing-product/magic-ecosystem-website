Where the price comes from

By Magic's estimate, a typical apartment needs 40–60 outlets and switches and 20–40 light fixtures. A hundred apartments add up to thousands of identical items, and they go to the factory together with orders from construction companies.

Boundaries

  • Goods are sold by whoever already serves the resident.
  • Returns, delivery and warranty stay with the seller.
  • Residents' money never passes through Magic.
  • The developer keeps its margin on pre-handover changes.
