Savings screen

For every line item: what you paid before, what you pay now and what made the difference

Four sources of savings the module is built on

Purchase price

Every invoice is compared with the pooled-order price and the landed cost. For a developer, materials are the largest line of direct costs, so the main impact lies in the price of what gets bought.

Contractor invoices

Each progress claim is checked against work verified on site before payment.

The cost of money

Deferred payment terms and a bank instrument tied to the deal count as a separate line of impact. Cash stays in the business instead of going to the factory as a prepayment.

Your own price base

Factory quotes and actual deliveries that build up in Magic deals.

The other half of savings is your revenue

The module looks at the revenue side too. It shows:

  • inquiries and deals stuck without a reply, and hands them to AI sales agents;
  • services your company can offer its own customers through banks: installments, mortgages, insurance;
  • fit-out and services that buyers currently look for on their own.

No payment leaves without a human

The card itself restricts supplier, category and amount. A suspicious payment goes to approval before it is made, not sorted out after the fact.

Amounts, limits and savings are calculated by code with auditable rules. AI prepares the proposal, a person makes the decision.

Magic doesn't hold funds, doesn't lend and never sees card numbers. Payments move between your company, its bank and the payee. The platform sits beside that path, not in it.

Savings with a calculation you can check

Source data comes from your company's invoices and payments, not from anyone's word. Impact is measured on two separate bases, purchase price and spend discipline, and adds up across four layers:

  1. 1

    Direct savings

    Price, freight, cost of money.

  2. 2

    Finance team workload

    Hours moved from people to the system.

  3. 3

    Prevented losses

    Duplicates, errors, contractor overpayments.

  4. 4

    Management layer

    Decisions made earlier because the numbers were visible earlier.

Every amount traces back to its source document. We don't promise a savings percentage up front: the number comes from the pilot, on your data.

FAQ

Do we need to switch banks?

No. Magic is designed to work with the banks where your company already has accounts and credit lines, and shows terms at other banks alongside.

Can Magic see my money?

Magic sees invoices, limits and budgets. The money stays in your company's accounts at your bank.

How much will we save?

The number comes from the pilot, on your invoices. We don't promise a percentage up front.

Does the module fit a company outside construction?

Yes. It is designed for any company with purchasing and supplier invoices.

Can we start without cards?

Yes. The pilot starts with invoice matching and the savings screen. Cards come later.
