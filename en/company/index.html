How we work

The customer's numbers.

Impact is measured on the customer's own invoices, not promised in advance.

AI does the work, code does the math, people decide.

Neutrality.

Magic holds no inventory and sells no goods of its own. Offers are compared by landed cost, and the comparison rules are set in the agreement with the customer.

Customer data stays theirs.

What we share is the technology.

Every side wins.

Factories get volume, banks get deal flow, customers get the price, specialists get the work.
