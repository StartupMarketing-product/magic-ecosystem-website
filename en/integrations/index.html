A Magic button next to every invoice

Your CFO approves the invoice in the accounting system they already use. Right next to it they see the pooled order price and the financing terms, and payment goes out through their own bank. No switching between systems.

Any system. One deal format

API

Direct two-way integration: orders, statuses and documents flow automatically.

XML, SOAP, EDI

Structured messages in a format your system already understands.

Files and SFTP

Scheduled exchange where nothing else is available.

Webhooks and polling

Events reach Magic instantly or are pulled on a schedule.

Email and operator

If a system has no interface, a Magic operator performs and records the step. You see the same screen.

Every system has its own adapter, while the whole platform runs on one internal model of the order, shipment, payment and document. So an order placed through an API and an order placed by phone look the same in the deal.

An integration is measured by the share of turnover that flows through it.

Magic measures an integration by the share of the client's turnover that flows through it. The systems with the most transactions connect first: accounting systems, banks and procurement.
