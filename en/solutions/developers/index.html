Sales, construction and a developer's money in one system.

AI agents handle unit sales and buyer relations. Procurement goes out to a factory tender. The site itself tells you which material it needs and when. Buyers see an estimated mortgage amount early on, and the credit decision stays with the bank.

Where developers lose money

A reserved unit waits on the bank.

Before the reservation, the mortgage module shows the buyer and the sales team the amount banks are likely to consider.

Materials are bought in small lots.

The developer's request goes into a pooled order and out to a factory tender.

Site deviations surface too late.

Progress on site turns straight into a requirement, an order and a budget line.

Buyers leave after handover.

Fit-out, finishing and service stay inside the developer's ecosystem at the pooled-order price.

Revenue after the sale

Fit-out, finishing and service for buyers under the developer's brand keep bringing in revenue after handover. The margin on pre-handover change requests stays with the developer.
