What the pilot tests

  • Achievable savings on the company's purchases and invoices.
  • Participation of factories, banks and carriers.
  • The economics of pooling orders.
  • The delivery route to the site.
  • The connection to the company's ERP or accounting system.
  • Measurable impact for the company.

Pilot boundaries

Payment for goods does not pass through Magic. Settlement runs through your bank.

Goods are not taken onto Magic's balance sheet.

A commitment to a factory, bank or carrier arises only after you sign.

Your company's data stays yours and is not visible to other participants.

Questions

Do we need to change suppliers or systems?

No. The pilot runs inside your systems, and the alternative is shown next to your current terms.

How long does a pilot last?

The timeframe is set in the pilot agreement and depends on the module.

What if we're not satisfied with the results?

The partner decides whether to continue, based on the measured results.

What are the pilot terms?

A Magic manager will go over them with you in a personal conversation.
