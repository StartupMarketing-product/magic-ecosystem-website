Lines that don't move

The specification stays with the designer.

Magic shows options within the chosen design and doesn't change the standard.

Selection runs on equal terms.

Importers take part in the tender on the same footing as everyone else and don't see other participants' commercial data. Ranking rules are the same for every participant and are set out in the agreement.

The bank makes the credit decision.

Magic prepares the deal and the documents. Approval comes from the bank itself.

Money does not pass through Magic. Payments flow between the client, their bank and the seller. Magic coordinates the deal, does not buy the goods and does not take them onto its balance sheet.
