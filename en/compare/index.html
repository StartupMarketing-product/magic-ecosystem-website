The market solves these tasks one by one. Magic runs them as one deal.

Company spend, procurement, construction progress, mortgages, valuation, the homebuyer and sales automation. Each task has its own category of strong solutions on the market. In Magic, they are modules of one platform on a shared AI core, and the output of one module becomes the input for the next.

Magic sits where these parts meet. Construction progress drives demand for procurement, procurement feeds prices to the finance module, and an apartment sale opens up the mortgage, the valuation and the buyer with their renovation.

What Magic connects in one deal

Pooled orders from independent companies

Requests from many construction companies combine into one factory batch. Each company's name, volume and price stay hidden from the others.

Direct factory tender

Pooled volume goes straight to manufacturers for bidding.

Full price to the site

The price is calculated with freight, duties and customs clearance, delivered to your site, not as it stands in the quote.

The bank inside the deal

Letters of credit, guarantees, credit and deferred payment terms are arranged in the same deal as the goods.

Carrier tender and delivery control

International shipping is put out to tender and tracked all the way to the site.

Quality based on facts

Inspection, claims and factory ratings are built on real deliveries.

One system runs the entire deal: factory tender, letter of credit, freight, customs, inspection, delivery and the invoice into your accounting system.

The rules Magic runs on

Neutrality

Magic holds no inventory and sells nothing of its own. Offers are compared on landed cost, and the comparison rules are set out in the contract with the client.

Every AI action logged

You see what the agent did, when and on what basis.

Audit-ready math

Savings break down to the original invoice, and rerunning the calculation gives the same result.

Freedom

You can switch off any connection yourself, at any time.

Deal languages

Agents talk to factories and clients in Hebrew, English, Russian and Chinese.
