The thesis

One platform instead of seven categories.

Finance, procurement, construction, mortgages, valuation, the apartment buyer and automation are each served in the market by a separate category of solutions. Magic brings them together into one deal.

No inventory on the balance sheet.

The goods are not bought by Magic, payment for them does not pass through the platform, and credit comes from the bank.

The moat is terms, not code.

Factory pricing, bank terms and supply data build up with every deal.

One core for seven modules.

The architecture is designed so that each new module and each new market launches on a foundation already in place.

Stage.

The model, the architecture and the impact measurement methodology are ready. The first pilots are launching in Israel.

The flywheel

Three loops: procurement volume, the apartment buyer, enterprise customers. Every new company strengthens the negotiating position with factories and banks for everyone else.

  1. 1

    Volume

    More companies in the order → stronger negotiating position with factories, banks and carriers → lower landed cost → more companies.

  2. 2

    Buyers

    Developer sales → homebuyer → mortgage and valuation → fit-out → buyer demand flowing into the same factory order.

  3. 3

    Enterprise clients

    Large companies where Magic runs enterprise automation connect Magic modules inside the same systems → new volume in the pooled order.
